How to play

  • Spawn: click anywhere on land during the countdown to pick your start.
  • Expand & attack: left-click wilderness or an enemy. You send the Send % of your troops, and they push toward where you clicked. Click again to steer.
  • Right-click (long-press on phones) any tile for every option: boats, paradrops, missiles, sabotage, alliances, emotes, pings.
  • Build from the bar at the bottom (or hotkeys 1-8, Q-T, A-H). Research Labs give science, which unlocks tiers 2-4.
  • Abilities: Z Blitz, X Entrench, C Rally. V recalls attacks. Space jumps home. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
  • Terrain matters: mountains, jungle and rivers are slow and easy to defend. Bunkers and Fortresses make land much harder to take.
  • Fog of war: enemy structures are hidden unless they are near your land, a Watchtower or a Radar. Enemy troop counts are estimates unless you are allied, spying, or have a Radar.
  • Diplomacy: up to 3 allies. Breaking an alliance gives them a 10-second warning and marks you a traitor for 90s.
  • Win by holding the target share of the world's land, by being the last one standing, or by having the most land when time runs out.
